FISHBOWL

A planted aquarium you look into rather than play.

The tank lives through a ten-minute day, from a grey dawn through
bright midday light and a warm sunset to the blue of a moonlight LED
while the fish sleep.


TO RUN

    Double-click Fishbowl.exe. Nothing needs installing.

    Keep it in this folder, beside its .dll files.

    Windows may say it protected your PC, because this build isn't
    signed. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".


WHAT YOU CAN DO

    Click               Sprinkle food
    Right-click         Tap the glass
    Rest the cursor     Meet a fish
    T                   Skip to the next time of day
    [ and ]             Slow down or speed up the day
    R                   Follow your real clock
    M                   Mute
    F12                 Save a picture
    H                   Show the help card


WHERE THINGS ARE KEPT

    The tank remembers its fish, and how much they have come to trust
    you, in %APPDATA%\Fishbowl. Pictures from F12 land in the snaps
    folder there.

    To remove Fishbowl, delete this folder, and that one too if you
    don't want the fish to remember you.
